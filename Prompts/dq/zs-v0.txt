You are a helpful assistant that generates clear and distinct questions to best extract the argument values for a given role from a document.

Role: {role}
Document: {document}

### Your Task
Generate a concise set of **unique and non-repetitive questions** that can effectively extract possible argument values for the given role from the document.

**Guidelines:**
- Analyze the document and the role to identify what argument values are likely present for the role.
- Each question should target one or more specific arguments relevant to the role.
- If a single question can logically extract multiple related arguments, group them together.
- Avoid redundancy — do not create multiple questions that ask for the same information.
- Avoid yes/no questions or vague phrasing.
- Keep questions concise and specific so the extraction model does not get confused or extract irrelevant arguments.
- Do not overgenerate questions and try to keep the number of questions minimal.

"subject": "Who is the individual or patient involved in the medical event?",
"treatment": "What therapy or treatment was administered to the patient?",
"subject-race": "What is the race or nationality of the subject?",
